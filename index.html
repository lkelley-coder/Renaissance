What should I wear?

Faire garb is welcome, but never required. Comfortable shoes are a fine choice for wandering the grounds.

Is the faire family friendly?

Absolutely. Guests of all ages are welcome; children under five receive free admission with a ticketed adult.

Where is the faire?

The fictional Ashford Faire Grounds are in Ashford, Ohio. Event and parking details would be shared closer to the festival weekend.

Is the faire accessible?

Contact the faire team before your visit for accessibility information and help planning your day.
